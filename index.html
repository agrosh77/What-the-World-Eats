Discover the Geography of Food

What's on your plate, and how did it get there? Every food has a story shaped by geography, history, agriculture, trade, and the people who grow, prepare, and share it. From everyday staples to ingredients that have traveled across oceans, food connects us in ways we might not always recognize.

Explore our three interactive maps to discover what the world eats, uncover patterns across countries and cultures, and follow the fascinating journeys of foods around the globe.
